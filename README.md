# Enterprise Generative AI Software: Microeconomic Industry Review

A Microeconomics (ECON102) course project by Shun-Min Tang at StraighterLine. The report evaluates enterprise AI application software, focusing on an application startup that buys access to foundation models rather than training its own frontier model.

## Questions and methods
- Examine demand, supply, production costs, and market structure.
- Distinguish foundation-model providers from application vendors.
- Separate reported estimates from illustrative planning assumptions.
- Evaluate a 2027–2028 revenue scenario and a financing-cost example.

## Scenario calculations checked
With a 2026 revenue index of 100, assumed annual usage growth of 30% and price declines of 10% produce revenue indices of 117 in 2027 and 136.9 in 2028. These are hypothetical scenario outputs, not observed revenues or published forecasts.

Using the report's assumptions, two annual salaries of $135,980 plus $20,000 annual interest require approximately $486,600 annual revenue at a 60% contribution margin, before other costs. An assumed elasticity of -1.5 and a 10% price increase imply a 15% quantity reduction and a 6.5% revenue decline using the report's discrete approximation.

## Conclusion in the submitted report
The report recommends selective investment in applications with paying customers, measurable savings, and controlled usage costs. Its B rating is an assignment judgment, not a standardized credit rating.
